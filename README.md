# NONLINEAR – Space Travel Edition

**Science doesn't have a type.**

This repository contains the website for NONLINEAR Space Travel, the first edition of NONLINEAR.

---

## What is NONLINEAR?

NONLINEAR is a science-communication and product brand. It is a creative, physics-based invention studio that helps young girls aged 11–14, invent things on their own.

It aims to fill the gap between science toys for primary-school girls and the science opportunities that come later in education.

Users don't follow fixed instructions. Instead, they discover physical principles, modify a working starter object, and invent useful or desirable products of their own.

---

## Educational Philosophy

NONLINEAR focuses on three priorities:

1. **Scientific Identity:** Experiencing what it feels like to be a scientist, engineer, or inventor.
2. **Scientific Skills:** Experimenting independently, solving problems, and making evidence-based decisions.
3. **Scientific Knowledge:** Discovering the principles behind the experiments.

### DISCOVER → UNDERSTAND → INVENT

Every mission follows the same approach.

When an experiment gives a surprising result, a short illustrated **"WAIT, WHY?" Science Card** explains the science in 50 words or fewer.

> Experience science first. Understand it second. Make something your own.

## First Edition: Space Travel

The first kit has a rocket and space theme. It is designed to feel like an influencer PR package rather than a school experiment set.

It comes with a personalized welcome letter, a name badge / role card, and three missions.

### The Missions

| Mission | Topic |
|---------|-------|
| **01 — The Material Edit** | Material science & engineering: compare five materials and choose the right one for an idea. |
| **02 — The Design Lab** | CAD & 3D printing: design your own component and receive it 3D-printed by mail. |
| **03 — The Invisible World** | Optics & polarization: reveal a hidden message on a seemingly blank card. |
